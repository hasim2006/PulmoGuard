# 🔬 Domain 02: Microbiology & Antibiotic Resistance

This domain explores bacterial pathogens responsible for lower respiratory infections (*Streptococcus pneumoniae*, *Klebsiella pneumoniae*, *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*), antimicrobial resistance mechanisms, and pathogen-host biochemical interactions.

---

## 📄 Key Papers & Publications

### 1. Antibiotic Resistance and Evolutionary Adaptation in Microbial Pathogens
- **Journal:** *Cell*
- **Local File:** [`Antibiotic-Resistance_cell.pdf`](Antibiotic-Resistance_cell.pdf)
- **Key Themes:** Horizontal gene transfer, $\beta$-lactamase mutation dynamics, efflux pump overexpression, and the clinical implications of multidrug-resistant pneumonia.

### 2. Lysozyme Counteracts $\beta$-Lactam Antibiotics by Promoting Cell Wall Remodeling
- **Journal:** *Cell Host & Microbe / Cell Press*
- **Local File:** [`Lysozyme-Counteracts-Beta-Lactam-Antibiotics-by-Promo.pdf`](Lysozyme-Counteracts-Beta-Lactam-Antibiotics-by-Promo.pdf)
- **Key Themes:** How innate respiratory enzymes like lysozyme interact with antimicrobial therapy in the pulmonary mucus lining, modulating bacterial lysis and survival.

### 3. The Unexpected Effects of the Combination of Antibiotics
- **Journal:** *Cell Press*
- **Local File:** [`The-Unexpected-Effects-of-the-Combination-of-Antib.pdf`](The-Unexpected-Effects-of-the-Combination-of-Antib.pdf)
- **Key Themes:** Drug-drug interactions, antagonism vs. synergy in combination antibiotic regimens for treating severe hospital-acquired pneumonia.

### 4. (p)ppGpp Regulatory Signaling and Stringent Response in Bacteria
- **Journal:** *Cell Press*
- **Local File:** [`Retraction-Notice-to--(p)ppGpp-Controls-Bacterial-.pdf`](Retraction-Notice-to--(p)ppGpp-Controls-Bacterial-.pdf)
- **Key Themes:** Second messenger signaling during nutrient starvation and antibiotic stress responses in bacterial persistence.

### 5. Electron Cryo-Microscopy Structure of Viral Pathogens
- **Journal:** *Cell*
- **Local File:** [`Electron-Cryo-microscopy-Structure-of-Ebola-Virus-.pdf`](Electron-Cryo-microscopy-Structure-of-Ebola-Virus-.pdf)
- **Key Themes:** High-resolution cryo-EM structural determination of viral nucleoproteins, illustrating viral envelope interaction and systemic pathogenesis.
