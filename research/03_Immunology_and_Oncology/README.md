# 🛡️ Domain 03: Immunology & Oncology

This domain examines host immune surveillance, alveolar dendritic cell activation, cytotoxic natural killer (NK) cell dynamics, and tumor metastasis pathways.

---

## 📄 Key Papers & Publications

### 1. NK Cells Stimulate Recruitment of cDC1 into the Tumor and Infection Microenvironment
- **Journal:** *Cell*
- **Local File:** [`NK-Cells-Stimulate-Recruitment-of-cDC1-into-the-Tu.pdf`](NK-Cells-Stimulate-Recruitment-of-cDC1-into-the-Tu.pdf)
- **Key Themes:** Chemokine production ($CCL5, XCL1$) by Natural Killer cells orchestrating the cross-priming capacity of type 1 conventional dendritic cells (cDC1) in mucosal and alveolar defense.

### 2. A Hematogenous Route for Medulloblastoma Leptomeningeal Metastasis
- **Journal:** *Cell*
- **Local File:** [`A-Hematogenous-Route-for-Medulloblastoma-Leptomeni.pdf`](A-Hematogenous-Route-for-Medulloblastoma-Leptomeni.pdf)
- **Key Themes:** Circulating tumor cells utilizing the hematogenous vascular route rather than contiguous cerebrospinal fluid dissemination, relevant to differential diagnosis of pulmonary secondary metastases vs. consolidation.
