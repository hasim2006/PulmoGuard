## 🧬 Scientific Overview: The Biology of Pneumonia

Pneumonia is an acute lower respiratory tract infection primarily affecting the microscopic air sacs (alveoli). When bacterial or viral pathogens infiltrate the pulmonary parenchyma:
1. **Inflammatory Cascade:** Alveolar macrophages release pro-inflammatory cytokines ($IL-1\beta, TNF-\alpha, IL-6$).
2. **Exudation & Consolidation:** Increased capillary permeability leads to cellular debris, neutrophils, and fibrin fluid filling the alveolar spaces.
3. **Radiographic Correlate:** On a normal chest radiograph, aerated lungs appear radiolucent (dark/black). In pneumonia, fluid-filled consolidated tissue increases X-ray attenuation, appearing as radio-opaque (cloudy white) infiltrates with characteristic air bronchograms and loss of normal cardiac/diaphragmatic silhouettes.
